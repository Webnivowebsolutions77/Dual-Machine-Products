About Dual Machine Products Limited

Established Canadian precision manufacturing company with 70+ years of custom machining expertise.

Mississauga, Ontario Headquarters

Experienced Custom Machining

Dual Machine Products has more than 70 years of experience in custom machining and the manufacturing of precision turned screw machine parts. Our experience and technical expertise have allowed us to develop strong relationships with our customers over the years, serving long-term, satisfied clients across Canadian and U.S. markets.

We Meet Complicated Production Requirements

At Dual Machine Products we are experts in custom CNC machining as well as in a number of other machining services. Our cutting-edge production equipment, facilities, and quality control programs allow us to create a variety of screw machine parts from simple designs to the toughest, most complicated specifications. We pride ourselves on staying current with industry trends and technology.

We Are More Than a Machine Shop

Dual Machine Products is proud of our highly qualified team and state-of-the-art production facilities. We have over 100 diversified high-production machines which allow us to meet a wide variety of customer specifications. Our tooling is produced in-house by our team of experienced tool makers—a decisive advantage over competitors. We offer full secondary operations and assembly to accurately create delicate or complicated assemblies.
